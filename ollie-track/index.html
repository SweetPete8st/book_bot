OllieTrack Tips

Best results: Keep the phone in a front or side pocket, or use a tight mount. Loose bags add noise.

Ollie detection: Looks for a strong pop followed by a short freefall window. Adjust Sensitivity if it misses soft pops or counts too many curbs.

Permissions: Motion & Location are required. On iPhone you must allow them when prompted (one-time).

Privacy: Everything stays in your browser’s localStorage. No accounts, no servers, no tracking.

Built for one-click Netlify Drop. Works offline after first load.
